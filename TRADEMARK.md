# Tenda trademark policy

The code in this repository is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`). The
Tenda **name and marks are not**. Apache-2.0 §6 grants no trademark rights, and
this document states plainly what that means so nobody has to infer it.

## What is not licensed

- The name **Tenda**, alone or as part of another name.
- The Tenda logo, wordmark and app icon, in any colour or variation.
- The domain `tenda.so` and any confusingly similar domain.
- The Tenda app listings and their store presence.

## What you may do

- Use, modify, run and redistribute the code, under Apache-2.0.
- Run your own instance, including commercially, **under your own name**.
- Say truthfully that your software is *"built on Tenda"* or *"a fork of
  Tenda"* — nominative reference to describe origin is expressly permitted by
  Apache-2.0 §6.
- Use the name in commentary, documentation, research and comparison.

## What you may not do

- Name, brand or market your fork as Tenda, or as anything close enough to be
  mistaken for it.
- Use the Tenda logo or wordmark in your fork's UI, store listing or site.
- Imply that your deployment is operated, endorsed or supported by Tenda.

If you run a fork, remove the Tenda name and marks from the interface and
release it under your own brand. The code is yours to use; the identity is how
users tell your deployment apart from ours, and that distinction protects them
as much as it protects us.

Questions, or want permission for something not covered here? Ask us.
